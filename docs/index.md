# Digital and Communication Technologies
Welcome to the Digital and Communication Technologies units for stage 4 Mandatory Technology. These units are designed to provide students with an understanding of how digital technologies work, and how they can be used to solve real-world problems. The units cover a range of topics, including programming, electronics, and web development for year 7 and 8.

1. [**Crack The Code**](crack_the_code/index.md): This unit introduces students to the basics of coding web pages in HTML and CSS. 
2. [**Illuminate**](illuminate/index.md): This unit combines woodwork with electronics and programming to create a series of light displays.
3. [**Digital Pet**](digital_pet/index.md): This unit introduces students to the basics of electronics, networks, data and programming. It will be used from 2026 onwards and aligns with the [NSW Technology 7-8 Syllabus (2026)](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/content/stage-4/fa8e5fb98c).

!!! Note
    **Crack the Code** and **Illuminate** are based off the older 2019 NSW Mandatory Technology Syllabus. They would need to be adapted to align with the new syllabus if they were to continue being used beyond 2025. The **Digital Pet** unit is designed to align with the new syllabus.
