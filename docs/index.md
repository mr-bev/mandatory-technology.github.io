# Digital and Communication Technologies
Welcome to the Digital and Communication Technologies units at Killarney Heights High School [(KHHS)](https://killarney-h.schools.nsw.gov.au/). These units are designed to provide students with an understanding of how digital technologies work, and how they can be used to solve real-world problems. The units cover a range of topics, including programming, electronics, and web development for year 7 and 8.

1. [**Crack The Code**](crack_the_code/index.md): This unit introduces students to the basics of coding web pages in HTML and CSS. 
2. [**Illuminate**](illuminate/index.md): This unit combines woodwork with electronics and programming to create a series of light displays.
3. [**Digital Pet**](digital_pet/index.md): This unit introduces students to the basics of electronics, networks, data and programming. It will be used from 2026 onwards and aligns with the [NSW Technology 7-8 Syllabus (2026)](https://curriculum.nsw.edu.au/learning-areas/tas/technology-7-8-2023/content/stage-4/fa8e5fb98c).

!!! Note
    **Crack the Code** and **Illuminate** will not be used after 2025 for [KHHS](https://killarney-h.schools.nsw.gov.au/) and will be replaced by **Digital Pet** following the new syllabus.
