# Overview

The Illuminate unit is a blended program that combines woodwork, electronics and programming to provide students with hands-on experience in creating digital products. The unit aims to develop students' skills in problem-solving, critical thinking, and creativity while also providing them with foundational knowledge and skills needed to pursue further studies in technology-related fields.

This program is planned to end in 2025 after which the new 2023 syllabus will be implemented. 

## Objectives

To build and decorate a box to house electronics and program a micro-controller to display LEDs sequences. 

## Safety Notes

- Leather shoes and aprons are required when in the workshop. If either are missing the student will not be able to participate in the workshop.
- Always wear safety goggles when using power tools.
- Students can only operate the disc sander and drill press. All other electrical tools must be operated by a teacher.
